The problem

Many local profiles were set up once and never touched again.

The top three spots on the map don't always go to the best businesses. Google looks at how close you are to the searcher, how complete your profile is and what people say about you.

✕

Wrong or missing info

Old hours, the wrong category, no services listed, and a handful of blurry photos.

✕

Few, old reviews

Happy customers often don't think to leave one, so the last review is from 2023.

✕

Reviews nobody answered

Unanswered reviews, especially bad ones, tell searchers nobody's minding the shop.

✕

No time to keep it up

You run a business. Posting updates to Google every week isn't on the list.

FAQ

Good questions.

How long until I see results?

The Fix-It work takes about 10 days to 6 weeks, because Google, Apple and Bing each check changes on their own time: a Google postcard can take up to 14 days, and an edit can wait up to 30 days for approval. Your before-and-after report comes about 6 to 10 weeks after you sign up, once Google has a whole month of numbers. Where you show on the map depends on how close the searcher is, how well your profile matches the search, your reviews and who you are up against, and it changes slowly. Nobody can promise a ranking, and we don't.

Do I need to give you my Google password?

No, and no codes either. You add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile from your own account, which takes about two minutes. You can remove us anytime.

I don't have a Google profile yet. Can you help?

Yes. You create and verify the profile from your own account, with us beside you. Google does the verifying, often by video or by a postcard that can take up to 14 days.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Locally owned businesses in and around Montgomery, up I-65 to Clanton, Calera and Montevallo, and west to Tuscaloosa and Bessemer: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, lawn care, pest control, auto repair, salons, dentists and the like. If people search "near me" for what you do, we can help. We don't work with franchises, and we never buy or fake reviews. For dental and medical offices, a review reply never confirms that someone is a patient.

What do I have to do?

For the Fix-It: claim or verify your listing with us beside you (you enter any codes yourself), add us as a manager, answer a few questions about your services, and hand out the review cards. If you choose a monthly plan, OK each review reply in writing, send a few photos when you have them and tell us about specials or events. We do the rest.
