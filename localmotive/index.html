FAQ

Good questions.

How long until I see results?

Fixes to your profile can show up within a few weeks of the Fix-It. Climbing the map usually takes 2–3 months of steady activity and new reviews, depending on how competitive your town is. Nobody can promise a ranking, and we don't.

Do I need to give you my Google password?

No. You add us as a manager on your Google Business Profile from your own account, which takes about two minutes. You can remove us anytime.

I don't have a Google profile yet. Can you help?

Yes. Setup includes creating and verifying the profile. Verification is done by Google, often by video or postcard, and we walk you through it.

What kinds of businesses do you work with?

Locally owned businesses in and around Montgomery, up I-65 to Clanton, Calera and Montevallo, and west to Tuscaloosa and Bessemer: HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, lawn care, pest control, auto repair, salons, dentists and the like. If people search "near me" for what you do, we can help. We don't work with franchises, and we never buy or fake reviews.

What do I have to do?

For the Fix-It: add us as a manager, answer a few questions about your services, and hand out the review cards. If you choose a monthly plan, send a few photos when you have them and tell us about specials or events. We do the rest.
