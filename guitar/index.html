    How to read this exercise

    High e is the top line; low E is the bottom. The number is the fret, and 0 is an open string. Each panel is one 4/4 bar. Counts show when to start a note; a longer column means hold it longer. R means rest.

    h = hammer onto the next marked note; p = pull off; s = slide; b = bend toward the written fret's pitch; ~ = vibrato. Rhythm exercises use D = down, U = up, X = muted attack, and – = silent hand motion.

    Use Hear exercise for the selected phrase. Backing jam follows the full chord progression and does not automatically play the whole lead line.

    Install, backup & privacy

    On iPhone, open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen. Turn on Open as Web App when that option appears. Open the app online once to cache the course.

    Notes, completion and practice settings are saved in this browser only, not to an account. Safari and a Home Screen app may have separate storage. Clearing website data can remove progress. Export a backup before switching devices or browsers.

    No login, analytics, microphone access or third-party audio downloads. Lessons and synthesized audio work offline after caching. The app does not assess your live guitar playing.

    When an update is ready, choose Update course after your practice. Your saved progress stays in place.
