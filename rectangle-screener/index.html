How the screen works

What counts as a rectangle

  • Flat resistance and flat support — not a rising or falling channel, triangle, or wedge.
  • Both lines tested at least twice, with price travelling across the box between tests.
  • Daily closes stay inside the box: wicks may poke through, and a close just past a line or a brief one- or two-day false breakout that snaps straight back is tolerated.
  • At least three weeks long, and tall enough to matter relative to the stock's normal daily range.

What makes the list

  • Inside range — the latest close is still inside an active rectangle. A recent false breakout that closed back inside is flagged.
  • Breakout ↑ / Breakdown ↓ — a decisive daily close above resistance or below support no more than 10 sessions ago, with no close back inside since.
  • Older breakouts and stocks with no clean range are left out.

Reading a row

  • Box — support to resistance, and the range height as a % of support.
  • Touches — distinct tests of resistance (T) and support (B).
  • Target — the classic measured move: the box height projected from the broken line.
  • Vol × — breakout-day volume against the prior 50-day average.
  • Quality — how textbook the box is: many alternating touches, tight flat lines, clean containment, balanced fill. 60+ is the cleanest tier; lower scores are looser ranges worth a second look.
